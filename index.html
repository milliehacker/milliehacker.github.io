I'm a 2nd-year PhD student in Linguistics at Michigan State University and an RA for the MI Diaries corpus project. I'm interested in phonological variation, especially intra-speaker variation. I am currently investigating how/when/why narrative speech (spontaneous story-telling) conditions shifts in speech style.

Things I've written class papers about and maintain passing interests in include: phonetic C-center stability, Super-Optimality, hiatus resolution in Ojibwe, word order shifting in Gadedansk ("Street Danish"), prefix loss in Hixkaryana, and Gradual Deletion.
